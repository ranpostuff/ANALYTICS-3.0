    About RescuePriority

    RescuePriority is the MCNHS Emergency Operations Center dashboard. It monitors 55 campus facilities in real time via an ESP32-linked Firebase backend, logs every emergency as a permanent incident record, and surfaces analytics on response activity.

    Data Source

    Facility status, incident records, and adviser assignments are read directly from Firebase Realtime Database (classrooms/ and incidents/) — nothing on this page is hard-coded or simulated.

    Help

    Quick guide to the Command Center

    Navigating RescuePriority

    • Command Center — the landing screen. Answers "what is happening right now" with KPIs, system status, the Live Campus Update carousel, recent incidents, and quick actions.
    • Dashboard — incident trend charts (by classroom, by status).
    • Campus Map — the interactive blueprint of all 55 facilities. Click any room for full details and emergency controls.
    • Incident Log — permanent history of every emergency, with a timeline for each.
    • Analytics — deeper charts: frequency over time, by classroom, by hour, by weekday, and by status.
